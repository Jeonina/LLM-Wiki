# Laryngeal squamous cell carcinoma (LSCC)

> An aggressive head-and-neck cancer arising from the laryngeal epithelium. Strongly associated with tobacco and alcohol exposure. Lacks reliable molecular biomarkers for early diagnosis and prognosis.

> [!abstract] 한눈에
> LSCC는 후두 상피에서 생기는 공격적인 두경부암으로 흡연과 음주가 주된 위험 요인이고, 조기 진단과 예후를 위한 분자 biomarker가 부족하다. nanopore 연구는 환자 74%에서 *TP53BP2*/*FBXO28*을 조절하는 simple-repeat 확장을 찾았다 ([[10-Summaries/liu-2025-nanopore-lscc-svs|Liu 2025]]).

## Definition

LSCC presents as a mass in the larynx with hoarseness, dysphagia, or dyspnea. Smoking is the dominant etiology; HPV plays a smaller role than in other head-and-neck SCCs.

## Why it matters

Early-stage LSCC has good survival but late-stage outcomes are poor. Molecular biomarkers (including somatic SVs detectable by long-read sequencing) are an active research area.
