# Andrew C. Adey

> Oregon Health & Science University. Extends [[combinatorial-indexing|combinatorial indexing]] to new modalities — most consequentially to bisulfite sequencing.

## Mentions

- **2026-08-13** — Corresponding author of [[mulqueen-2018-sci-met]] (sci-MET), which brought combinatorial indexing to single-cell whole-genome bisulfite sequencing using **cytosine-depleted transposome adaptors** that survive bisulfite treatment. The headline result was an alignment rate of 68 ± 8%, approaching bulk, against the 25 ± 20% typical of one-cell-per-well scWGBS.
