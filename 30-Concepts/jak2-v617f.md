# JAK2 V617F

> Recurrent valine-to-phenylalanine point mutation at codon 617 of the Janus kinase 2 (JAK2) gene; the most common driver of [[myeloproliferative-neoplasm]] and a precursor lesion in clonal hematopoiesis. Constitutively activates JAK2 → STAT signaling in a cytokine-independent manner.

## Definition

JAK2 normally requires cytokine receptor engagement (EPO, TPO, G-CSF, etc.) to signal. The V617F mutation in the JH2 pseudokinase domain releases the kinase's auto-inhibition, producing constitutive activation and downstream phosphorylation of STAT3, STAT5, and STAT1 ([[10-Summaries/izzo-2024-got-cha]]).

## Why it matters

JAK2V617F is found in:
- ~95% of polycythemia vera (PV) patients,
- ~50% of essential thrombocythemia (ET) and primary myelofibrosis (MF) patients,
- A substantial fraction of clonal-hematopoiesis-of-indeterminate-potential (CHIP) cases.

[[10-Summaries/izzo-2024-got-cha]] reframes JAK2V617F's effect on HSCs from "downstream cytokine inflammation" to **cell-intrinsic chromatin priming**: at the clonal-hematopoiesis stage, mutant HSCs already have:

- Increased gene accessibility for **NF-κB target genes** (TRAPPC9),
- Increased accessibility for **TGF-β superfamily** components (BMPR1B receptor, GDF10 ligand),
- Increased accessibility for **MMP15** (matrix remodeling),
- Decreased accessibility for stem/quiescence genes (FRY, HLF, PBX1),
- Increased **STAT1/5 motif accessibility** that is reversed by ruxolitinib.

These changes are visible *before* overt MPN — JAK2V617F primes the chromatin landscape ahead of the disease phenotype.

## Variants and refinements

- **Heterozygous JAK2V617F** — common; modest pro-inflammatory phenotype.
- **Homozygous JAK2V617F** — typically arises from copy-neutral loss of heterozygosity (UPD on chr 9p); more severe phenotype, NFKB1 and REL motifs specifically increased ([[10-Summaries/izzo-2024-got-cha]]).
- Pharmacologic targeting: ruxolitinib (JAK1/2 inhibitor) suppresses cytokine signaling and chromatin TF motif differences but **does not eliminate the mutated clone** ([[10-Summaries/izzo-2024-got-cha]]).

## Contested points

- Whether the cell-intrinsic pro-inflammatory chromatin program is *causal* for clonal expansion or *consequential* — the longitudinal Pt-01 PV→MF data point to early causality but cohort-scale evidence is limited.
- Mechanism connecting JAK2V617F kinase activity to chromatin remodeling at NF-κB and TGF-β loci is not nailed down (STAT-mediated? direct? pioneer factor recruitment?).

## Examples

- 21 JAK2V617F MPN samples (150,643 cells) profiled with [[got-cha]]: 38.1% genotyping rate, cell-intrinsic chromatin priming visible at clonal hematopoiesis ([[10-Summaries/izzo-2024-got-cha]]).
- Mutant cells preferentially distribute into erythroid–megakaryocytic and granulocyte–monocyte progenitors; lymphoid clusters remain ~92% wild-type.
