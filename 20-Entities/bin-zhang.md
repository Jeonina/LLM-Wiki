# Bin Zhang

> Associate Professor of Chemistry at **MIT**. Theoretical / computational lab specializing in polymer physics of chromatin and biomolecular condensates. Develops Hi-C-parameterized polymer models to study how chromatin structure and dynamics emerge from underlying biophysical forces.

## Selected work

- [[10-Summaries/qi-zhang-2021-nucleoli-coalescence]] — corresponding author; polymer-model demonstration that viscoelastic chromatin network arrests nucleoli coalescence, stabilizing the multi-droplet state.
- (Older, not yet ingested) — Di Pierro et al. 2016 *PNAS* — transferable polymer model of chromosome architecture; foundational for Hi-C → 3D structure inference.

## Why it matters for this wiki

The Zhang lab provides the **mechanistic polymer-physics layer** between Hi-C measurements and biophysical predictions. Qi & Zhang 2021 is the cleanest example of using a Hi-C-calibrated polymer model to explain why nuclear bodies show stable multi-droplet architectures rather than coalescing into single droplets — a key piece of the chromatin LLPS picture.
