# Rudolf Jaenisch

> Whitehead Institute / MIT geneticist whose lab pioneered transgenic mice, somatic-cell nuclear transfer, and induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC) reprogramming. Long-running interest in epigenetic regulation of pluripotency and reprogramming fidelity.

## Mentions in this wiki

- **2010-10-26** — Corresponding author on [[10-Summaries/creyghton-2010-h3k27ac-enhancers]], establishing H3K27ac as the active-enhancer mark and showing fibroblast→iPS reprogramming resets the enhancer landscape (Pearson 0.81 vs ES). The iPS line used was the lab's tetraploid-complementation-competent line (Wernig et al. 2007).
