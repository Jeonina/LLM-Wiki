# Steven Henikoff

> Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center / HHMI Investigator. Inventor of CUT&RUN (with Skene 2017), CUT&Tag (with Kaya-Okur 2019), and the methods derivative chain that has substantially replaced ChIP-seq.

## Mentions

- **2026-05-12** — Senior author of [[10-Summaries/janssens-2023-scicut-tag]] (sciCUT&Tag) and originator of CUT&Tag/CUT&RUN.
- **2026-10-07** — His group's CUT&Tag method and published CUT&Tag/CUT&RUN data are the subject and comparator of the independent benchmark [[10-Summaries/abbasova-2025-cut-tag-encode-benchmark]] (~54% ENCODE recall; no S/N advantage for H3K27ac).
- **2026-10-07** — corresponding author in [[10-Summaries/skene-2017-cut-and-run]] (introduced CUT&RUN: antibody-tethered pA-MNase cleavage and in situ release, ~1/10th ChIP sequencing depth, spike-in quantification).
- **2026-10-07** — lab's CUT&Tag datasets used as training data in [[10-Summaries/hu-2026-patty]] (K562 H3K27me3/H3K27ac CUT&Tag from Kaya-Okur 2019; residual open-chromatin bias found even with high-salt and CUTAC protocols).
- **2026-10-07** — Senior author of the 2019 study credited with introducing scCUT&Tag (pA-Tn5, ICELL8, H3K27me3/H3K4me2 in K562 and H1) in [[10-Summaries/wu-2026-sccut-tag-review]].
