# Oliver Stegle

> EMBL Heidelberg / DKFZ computational and statistical genomics lab. Works on single-cell and multi-omic statistical methods (e.g. the MOFA factor-analysis framework). Co-author on SDR-seq.

Coauthor of [[10-Summaries/kleshchevnikov-2022-cell2location]], the Bayesian deconvolution method for spatial transcriptomics.
