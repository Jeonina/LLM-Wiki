# Caleb A. Lareau

> MSKCC computational biologist; co-developed **mtscATAC-seq** (with Leif Ludwig, Nat Biotech 2021) for joint single-cell mtDNA genotyping + chromatin accessibility, and authored the **mgatk** mitochondrial variant-calling pipeline that powers most subsequent mtDNA single-cell work.
