# Clonal hematopoiesis

> Mosaic expansion of a hematopoietic stem cell clone carrying a somatic driver mutation, detectable in peripheral blood by sequencing. Common with age (~10–15% of adults over 70), increases risk of hematologic malignancy and cardiovascular disease, and serves as a precursor state to overt MPN / MDS / AML.

## Definition

A subset of mature blood cells share a somatic mutation traceable to a common HSC ancestor, typically detected as ≥2% VAF for canonical drivers. Most common drivers ([[10-Summaries/lars-2017-naturereviewsgenetics]]):

- **DNMT3A** — methyltransferase loss-of-function, ~40% of CHIP cases.
- **TET2** — demethylation enzyme loss-of-function, ~15%.
- **ASXL1**, **JAK2 V617F**, **SF3B1**, **TP53**, etc.

**Clonal Hematopoiesis of Indeterminate Potential (CHIP)** is the clinical term for ≥2% VAF of a leukemia-driver mutation without overt hematologic disease.

## Why it matters

- **Cancer risk**: ~0.5–1% per year transition rate to MDS / AML.
- **Cardiovascular risk**: independent risk factor for atherosclerosis; mechanism via inflammatory monocyte/macrophage phenotype.
- **Precursor state for** MPN drivers including JAK2 V617F ([[10-Summaries/franco-2024-nature]] explicitly profiled a JAK2V617F CH sample and showed cell-intrinsic chromatin priming *before* overt MPN).
- **Common with age**: detectable in ~10% of adults over 70.

## Variants and refinements

- **CHIP** — clinical diagnostic threshold (≥2% VAF of a leukemia driver).
- **CCUS** (clonal cytopenia of undetermined significance) — CHIP + cytopenias.
- **Pre-leukemic clonal expansions** — preceding overt AML/MDS by years.

## Contested points

- The right threshold (VAF, gene set) for clinical action.
- Whether CHIP-associated cardiovascular risk is causal or shared-risk-factor mediated.

## Examples

- DNMT3A R882H clones expanding in elderly individuals over decades.
- JAK2V617F CH detected via [[got-cha]] showing pre-disease chromatin priming ([[10-Summaries/franco-2024-nature]]).
