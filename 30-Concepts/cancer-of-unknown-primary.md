# Cancer of unknown primary (CUP)

> A cancer diagnosed by metastasis without an identifiable primary tumor. ~3–9% of cancer diagnoses; fourth most common cause of cancer-related death. Median survival 9 months without origin-targeted therapy; identifying the primary site dramatically improves outcomes.

> [!abstract] 한눈에
> CUP는 전이 병소로 진단되지만 원발 부위를 찾지 못한 암이다. 암세포가 전이 후에도 기원 조직의 DNA 메틸화 패턴을 유지하기 때문에, EPICUP 같은 메틸화 분류기로 원발 부위를 추정할 수 있다 ([[10-Summaries/kim-2017-methylation-memory-review|Kim 2017]]).

## Definition

CUP cases are heterogeneous: metastases of carcinomas, neuroendocrine tumors, lymphomas. Imaging, immunohistochemistry, and molecular profiling are used to identify the tissue of origin.

## Why it matters

- **EPICUP** (Moran et al. 2016) is a methylation-based classifier that identified the primary site in 188/216 (87%) of CUP patients with 99.6% specificity and 97.7% sensitivity ([[10-Summaries/kim-2017-methylation-memory-review|Kim 2017]]).
- Demonstrates that **DNA methylation is a reliable record of cell-type origin** even after metastatic spread — a striking example of [[30-Concepts/epigenetic-memory]] ([[10-Summaries/kim-2017-methylation-memory-review|Kim 2017]]).
- Methylation classifiers are being extended to clinical workflows (Heidelberg brain-tumor MNP classifier, AML methylation classifiers).
