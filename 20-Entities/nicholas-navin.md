# Nicholas Navin

> PI at MD Anderson Cancer Center; pioneer of single-cell DNA sequencing in cancer. Established droplet-microfluidics scDNA-seq workflows and led foundational work on punctuated clonal evolution in breast cancer ([[10-Summaries/navin-2011-sns-tumor-evolution]]).

## Profile

- Affiliation: Department of Genetics, MD Anderson Cancer Center
- Research focus: single-cell DNA sequencing of tumors, breast-cancer clonal evolution, chemoresistance, copy-number variation
- Key methodological contributions: scWGA optimization (early MDA), 10x CNV droplet platform development, copy-number phylogeny tooling

## Mentions

- **2026-05-19** — Co-author on the Navin lab's foundational SNS scDNA-seq paper ([[10-Summaries/navin-2011-sns-tumor-evolution]]) and TNBC chemoresistance work ([[10-Summaries/kim-2018-tnbc-chemoresistance]]).
