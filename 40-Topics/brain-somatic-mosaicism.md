# Brain somatic mosaicism

> Somatic mutations accumulating in human neurons and other brain cells throughout life. Fetal-progenitor SNVs (~200-400/cell) plus age-related accumulation produce a distinct mosaicism profile per neuron. Implicated in normal aging, Alzheimer's disease, autism, and brain tumors ([[10-Summaries/bizzotto-2022-brain-mosaicism-review]]; [[10-Summaries/lodato-2017-aging-neurons]]).
