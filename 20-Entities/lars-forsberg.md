# Lars A. Forsberg

> Uppsala University human geneticist; first author of the 2017 Nature Reviews Genetics review on mosaicism in health and disease. Major focus on age-related somatic mosaic chromosomal events (mLOY — mosaic loss of Y chromosome).

## Profile

Department of Immunology, Genetics and Pathology, Uppsala University; Science for Life Laboratory. Email lars.forsberg@igp.uu.se.

## Mentions

- **2026-05-11** — [[10-Summaries/forsberg-2017-mosaicism-review]]: first author of the mosaicism review.

## Positions and claims

- Holds that **every human is a complex genetic mosaic** — no two somatic cells likely share identical genomes ([[10-Summaries/forsberg-2017-mosaicism-review]]).
- Holds that **structural variants leave the largest mosaic genomic footprint** even though point mutations are more numerous.
