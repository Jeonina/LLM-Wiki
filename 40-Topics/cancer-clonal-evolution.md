# Cancer clonal evolution

> The accumulation of somatic mutations and clonal selection that shapes tumor heterogeneity, treatment response, and metastasis. Reconstructing tumor phylogenies from bulk and single-cell DNA data reveals the order of driver events, clonal sweep dynamics, and chemoresistance lineages ([[10-Summaries/navin-2011-sns-tumor-evolution]]; [[10-Summaries/kim-2018-tnbc-chemoresistance]]).
