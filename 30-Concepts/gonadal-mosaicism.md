# Gonadal mosaicism

> Subclass of [[40-Topics/somatic-mosaicism]] in which the mosaic mutation is present in the gonadal lineage (sperm or eggs) and therefore can be transmitted to multiple offspring even though the parent appears clinically unaffected. A major source of unexplained recurrence in apparently de novo dominant genetic disorders.

> [!abstract] 한눈에
> Gonadal mosaicism은 mosaic mutation이 정자나 난자 계열에 있어서, 겉보기에 정상인 부모가 여러 자녀에게 같은 변이를 물려줄 수 있는 상태다. 원시 생식세포가 분화하기 전에 생긴 post-zygotic mutation이 생식선까지 퍼지면서 생긴다 ([[10-Summaries/campbell-2015-mosaicism-review|Campbell 2015]]).

## Definition

A post-zygotic mutation that arises before primordial germ cell (PGC) differentiation (within the first ~15 mitotic divisions, by day 24 post-fertilization) can populate the gonad with mutated germ cells alongside (or instead of) somatic cells ([[10-Summaries/campbell-2015-mosaicism-review|Campbell 2015]]).

## Why it matters

- **Recurrence risk in apparently sporadic disorders**: an unaffected parent of an affected child may carry gonadal mosaicism. A second affected child is then not a coincidence but a transmission event.
- **Standard genetic counseling underestimates risk** unless gonadal mosaicism is considered for sibling-recurrence cases of apparently de novo dominant disorders.
- **Detection requires germ cell sampling** — invasive (testis/ovary biopsy) and rarely performed; bulk somatic DNA can miss it entirely.

## Forms

| Form | Where the mutation is | Phenotype and detection |
|---|---|---|
| Gonadal-only mosaicism | Confined to germline | Parent has normal somatic phenotype but transmits the mutation |
| Gonosomal mosaicism | Both soma and germline | Parent may have mild somatic phenotype + transmits to offspring |
| Male gonadal mosaicism | Sperm lineage | Detectable by sperm DNA analysis (non-invasive) |
| Female gonadal mosaicism | Egg lineage | Requires invasive ovarian biopsy; rarely confirmed in life |

## Evidence and examples

- Apparently sporadic Duchenne muscular dystrophy families with multiple affected siblings — gonadal mosaicism is now recognized as a major mechanism.

## Contested points

- Empirical recurrence risk estimates for specific disorders are uncertain — large family-history datasets are needed to quantify.
