# Jan Vijg

> Senior author whose lab studies somatic mutation and genome mosaicism in aging, with both single-cell and bulk single-molecule methods.

## Mentions

- **2026-10-07** — Corresponding author of [[10-Summaries/maslov-2022-smm-seq]] (SMM-seq: hairpin adapters plus pulse-RCA for bulk detection of rare somatic SNVs; reproduces the age-related rise in liver mutation frequency previously measured by the lab's single-cell approach).
- Senior author on [[10-Summaries/dong-2017-sccaller]] (SCcaller, single-cell SNV calling that addresses amplification and lysis artefacts).
- Co-author of the review [[10-Summaries/vijg-2020-cell]] on pathogenic mechanisms of somatic mutation and mosaicism in aging.
