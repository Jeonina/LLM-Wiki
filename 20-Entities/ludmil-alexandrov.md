# Ludmil B. Alexandrov

> UC San Diego faculty whose lab defined the modern framework for **mutational signatures** in cancer (COSMIC SBS catalog) and continues to develop high-accuracy sequencing methods. Founder of Acurion (formerly io9).

## Profile

Bioinformatics + cancer biology. Sanger-Cambridge alumnus (Stratton lab). UCSD Moores Cancer Center. Lab develops SigProfiler tools for signature decomposition.

## Mentions

- **2026-05-12** — Senior author of [[10-Summaries/nandi-2025-udseq]] (UDSeq, 2025 bioRxiv preprint).
- Earlier: senior author of foundational Alexandrov 2013 *Nature* paper defining the 30 COSMIC SBS signatures.
