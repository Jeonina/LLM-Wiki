# Bae et al. 2017 — pregastrulation vs neurogenesis mutational rates

> Thesis: somatic mutations accumulate at different rates and with different signatures in two distinct windows of human brain development — pregastrulation (early embryogenesis) and neurogenesis (cortical formation). Single-neuron MDA-based WGS resolves both windows and shows that mutation type and rate change between developmental stages, with implications for both neuronal diversity and disease predisposition.

## Key claims (abstract + intro)

- **Two developmental mutation windows** are detectable in single-neuron genomes: early pre-gastrulation embryonic and later cortical neurogenesis.
- **Rates and mechanisms differ** between windows: distinct mutation spectra reflect distinct mutational processes operating at each stage.
- These early mutations could **generate useful neuronal diversity** or **predispose individuals to later dysfunction** (e.g., focal epilepsy, neuropsychiatric disorders).
- Companion paper to Lodato et al. 2017 (aging neurons) — same *Science* issue. Together they bracket the lifespan of somatic mutation accumulation.

## Why this matters

Anchors the **developmental mutation timing** axis of the wiki. Establishes single-neuron WGS as a method capable of *timing* somatic mutations to specific embryonic windows — extending lineage tracing into early human development.
