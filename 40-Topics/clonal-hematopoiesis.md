# Clonal hematopoiesis

> Age-related expansion of mosaic blood-cell clones driven by recurrent somatic mutations (DNMT3A, TET2, JAK2 V617F, CALR, ASXL1). Confers elevated risk for cardiovascular disease and hematologic malignancy. The single-cell genomics field's most tractable mosaicism disease model ([[10-Summaries/franco-2024-nature]]; [[10-Summaries/anna-2019-nature]]).
