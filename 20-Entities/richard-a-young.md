# Richard A. Young

> Whitehead Institute / MIT transcription biologist. Foundational work on transcription factor regulatory circuits in mESCs (Oct4/Sox2/Nanog network), later coined the **super-enhancer** concept (Whyte/Hnisz 2013) using H3K27ac density as the operational definition, and more recently has driven the **transcriptional condensates** / LLPS framework.

## Mentions in this wiki

- **2010-10-26** — Co-author on [[10-Summaries/creyghton-2010-h3k27ac-enhancers]]. The H3K27ac+ active-enhancer partition established here is the operational basis for super-enhancer calling in subsequent Young-lab work (not yet ingested).
