# Gibson et al. 2019 — Chromatin LLPS

> Thesis: reconstituted chromatin undergoes **histone-tail-driven liquid–liquid phase separation (LLPS)** at physiological salt — both in vitro and when microinjected into cell nuclei. The condensates are dense, dynamic droplets whose properties are tuned by **linker histone H1** and conserved **inter-nucleosome linker lengths**. Histone acetylation by p300 dissolves the droplets; bromodomain-containing readers (e.g., BRD4) bind hyperacetylated chromatin and re-form a *distinct* phase, immiscible with unmodified chromatin droplets. This is the foundational biophysical model for understanding how the chromatin polymer itself organizes into the subnuclear compartments observed by microscopy.

## Key claims (from abstract)

- Reconstituted nucleosomal arrays phase-separate into liquid-like droplets at physiologic ionic strength — driven by histone N-terminal tails.
- **Microinjection into cell nuclei** reproduces droplet formation in vivo, indicating physiologic relevance.
- **Linker histone H1** promotes phase separation; **inter-nucleosome linker lengths** conserved across eukaryotes tune droplet density to match nuclear chromatin behavior.
- **p300 acetylation antagonizes LLPS** — dissolves droplets in vitro and reduces nuclear droplet formation.
- **Multi-bromodomain proteins (BRD4)** binding to acetylated chromatin establish a new phase-separated state, with distinct physical properties and **immiscibility** with unmodified chromatin droplets — mimicking nuclear chromatin subdomains.

## Why this matters for the wiki

- Establishes that chromatin organization into discrete subdomains is a **biophysical inevitability** of the polymer + modifications, not solely the product of protein factors. Provides the chemical-physics foundation for the **structural-physical axis of DNA locus state** (the 3c sub-axis in the user's framework).
- Connects the histone-modification language (H3K27ac, H3K4me, etc.) to a mechanistic picture: acetylation flips chromatin from one phase to another, and reader proteins establish phase identity.
- Frames downstream cancer-LLPS work ([[10-Summaries/ahn-2021-llps-cancer-looping]]) where oncogenic IDR fusions hijack the same machinery.

## Open questions (raised by this source)

- How does the in-vitro / microinjection LLPS picture quantitatively map to native chromatin where ATP, RNAs, and scaffolding proteins compete with H1?
- Where do the **boundaries between immiscible chromatin phases** fall in the genome? Are they congruent with TAD boundaries, A/B compartments, or LAD edges?
- Therapeutic angle: can LLPS-targeting strategies (e.g., 1,6-hexanediol probes, IDR-binding drugs) be developed without dissolving essential physiologic condensates?
