# Michael K. Rosen

> Professor of Biophysics and Howard Hughes Medical Institute investigator at **UT Southwestern Medical Center**. One of the founders of the modern biomolecular-condensate / LLPS field. His lab established the principles connecting multivalent weak interactions to phase separation and applied them to signaling complexes (e.g., T-cell receptor microclusters, NCK-N-WASP-actin), nuclear bodies, and chromatin organization.

## Selected work

- [[10-Summaries/gibson-2019-chromatin-llps]] — corresponding author; histone-tail-driven chromatin LLPS, H1/linker tuning, p300 dissolution, BRD4 phase switching.
- (Older, not yet ingested) — Li et al. 2012 *Nature* — phase transitions in multivalent signaling proteins; foundational LLPS paper.

## Why it matters for this wiki

Rosen's chromatin LLPS framework is the biophysical foundation for understanding the **mechanical/phase-separation sub-axis of DNA locus state**. The bromodomain reader hypothesis (acetylated chromatin recruits BRD4 → distinct phase) directly connects histone modifications to subnuclear compartment identity.
