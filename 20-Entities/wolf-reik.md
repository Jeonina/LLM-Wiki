# Wolf Reik

> Former director of the Babraham Institute; one of the founders of mammalian developmental epigenetics. Co-developer of scNMT-seq (with Stegle/Marioni) and longstanding contributor to genomic-imprinting biology.

## Profile

- Affiliation: Babraham Institute (Cambridge); Wellcome Sanger Institute; Altos Labs
- Research focus: DNA methylation dynamics in development, X-inactivation, imprinting, single-cell multi-omics
