# Siddharth S. Dey

> PI in the Department of Chemical Engineering and Center for Bioengineering at **UC Santa Barbara**. Specialty: single-cell multi-omic method development, with engineering emphasis on chemistry that supports joint readouts.

## Method lineage

- **Dey 2015** (Nat Biotech) — integrated genome and transcriptome sequencing of the same cell; a precursor to G&T-seq.
- **Rooijers 2019** (Nat Biotech, [[10-Summaries/rooijers-2019-scdamt-seq]]) — co-senior with Jop Kind; scDam&T-seq.
