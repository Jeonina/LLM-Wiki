# Menno P. Creyghton

> Chromatin biologist who first established **H3K27ac as the discriminating mark separating active from inactive enhancers** while a postdoc in the Jaenisch lab. Later established his own group focused on enhancer regulation in development and disease.

## Mentions in this wiki

- **2010-10-26** — First author on [[10-Summaries/creyghton-2010-h3k27ac-enhancers]]. ChIP-seq across mESC, NPC, proB, liver, fibroblasts and iPSCs; defined the H3K4me1+/H3K27ac+ active vs H3K4me1+/H3K27ac− "poised" partition that became standard. Note: his "poised" terminology was later renamed "primed" in the field; see [[30-Concepts/enhancer-states]].
