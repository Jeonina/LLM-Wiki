# Micro-C

> Micro-C is a Hi-C variant that fragments crosslinked chromatin into mononucleosomes with micrococcal nuclease before proximity ligation, yielding nucleosome-resolution contact maps ([[10-Summaries/hsieh-2015-micro-c]]).

## Definition

The original protocol uses MNase digestion to >95% mononucleosomes, biotin end-labelling, dilute ligation, exonuclease III removal of unligated ends and streptavidin pulldown; an interspecies mixing control estimated ~10% spurious ligations ([[10-Summaries/hsieh-2015-micro-c]]).

## Why it matters

- It fills the resolution gap between ~150-bp nucleosome mapping and ~1–4-kb restriction-enzyme Hi-C ([[10-Summaries/hsieh-2015-micro-c]]).
- In yeast it revealed gene-scaled chromosomal interaction domains and no periodic 30-nm-fibre signal in vivo ([[10-Summaries/hsieh-2015-micro-c]]).
- It captures long-range contacts such as centromere clustering poorly, so it complements rather than replaces Hi-C ([[10-Summaries/hsieh-2015-micro-c]]).
