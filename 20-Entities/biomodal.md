# biomodal

> A Cambridge, UK biotech (formerly Cambridge Epigenetix / CEGX) that develops bisulfite-free DNA methylation sequencing technologies. Sells the **duet multiomics solution evoC** kit which enables 6-base sequencing (G/A/T/C + 5mC + 5hmC) on Illumina platforms.

## Profile

Co-founded by Shankar Balasubramanian. The evoC chemistry powers 6-base-CUT&Tag and similar bulk and single-cell multi-modal DNA-modification methods.
