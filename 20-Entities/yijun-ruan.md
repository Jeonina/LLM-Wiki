# Yijun Ruan

> Originator of the paired-end-tag sequencing strategy and of [[chia-pet|ChIA-PET]] — the protein-anchored branch of genome-wide chromatin interaction mapping.

## Mentions

- **2026-08-13** — Senior author of [[li-2014-chia-pet]], the protocol-and-applications review of ChIA-PET. It is the definitional source in this corpus for protein-anchored interaction mapping, and for the statistic that underwrites the whole enhancer-to-gene enterprise: **over 40% of enhancers do not regulate their nearest promoter**.
- ChIA-PET's descendants (PLAC-seq, HiChIP) now appear in the single-cell literature as reference truth rather than as assays, because immunoprecipitation cannot be done on one cell ([[yu-2021-snaphic]]). (synthesis)
