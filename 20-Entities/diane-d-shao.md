# Diane D. Shao

> Pediatric neurologist–scientist at Boston Children's / Harvard; first author of the 2025 Nature Reviews Genetics review that frames the current scDNA-seq landscape. Associated with the [[20-Entities/christopher-walsh|Walsh lab]] focus on brain somatic mosaicism.

## Profile

Department of Neurology and Division of Genetics, Boston Children's Hospital and Harvard Medical School. Email Diane.Shao@childrens.harvard.edu.

## Mentions

- **2026-05-11** — [[10-Summaries/diane-2025-naturereviewsgenetics]]: first author of the keystone scDNA-seq review used as the methodological backbone for this wiki cluster.

## Positions and claims

- Holds that **scDNA-seq is now an umbrella term** covering both scWGA-based and single-molecule duplex approaches ([[10-Summaries/diane-2025-naturereviewsgenetics]]).
- Holds that **PTA + duplex sequencing + improved CNV callers** mark a generational transition that finally makes routine human-tissue mosaicism studies feasible ([[10-Summaries/diane-2025-naturereviewsgenetics]]).
