# Genosenium

> The accumulated somatic-mutation landscape of aged cells and tissues — the "aged genome" considered as a phenotype. Frames somatic mosaicism as a hallmark of aging analogous to telomere shortening or epigenetic drift (synthesis; term coined in [[10-Summaries/lodato-2017-aging-neurons|Lodato 2018]]; usage also in [[10-Summaries/vijg-2020-cell|Vijg 2020]]; [[10-Summaries/cagan-2022-nature|Cagan 2022]]).

> [!abstract] 한눈에
> Genosenium은 나이 든 세포와 조직에 쌓인 somatic mutation 전체를 하나의 표현형으로 보는 개념으로, 정상 신경세포에서 나이에 따라 mutation이 늘어나는 현상을 가리키려고 만들어졌다 ([[10-Summaries/lodato-2017-aging-neurons|Lodato 2018]]). 이 mutation 부담이 노화 관련 질환의 원인인지, 단지 biomarker인지는 아직 열린 질문이다 (synthesis).

## Definition

Coined by Lodato et al. for the slow, age-related accumulation of somatic mutations in normal human neurons ([[10-Summaries/lodato-2017-aging-neurons|Lodato 2018]]), "genosenium" denotes the cumulative mutation burden that distinguishes aged from young cells, together with the cell-type-specific selection pressures that shape which clones expand. Distinct from senescence (a cellular phenotype) — focuses on the genomic substrate.

## Why it matters

It treats the aged genome itself as a measurable phenotype, alongside telomere shortening and epigenetic drift, so that mutation burden and clonal expansion can be compared across tissues and species (synthesis).

## Evidence and examples

**Mutation rates**

- Somatic mutation rate scales with lifespan across mammals ([[10-Summaries/cagan-2022-nature|Cagan 2022]]).
- Aged human cells accumulate somatic SNVs at tissue-dependent rates. MDA single-neuron WGS estimated ~23 sSNVs/year in prefrontal cortex and ~40/year in dentate gyrus ([[10-Summaries/lodato-2017-aging-neurons|Lodato 2018]]).
- PTA later revised the PFC rate to ~16.5 sSNVs/year ([[10-Summaries/luquette-2022-neuron-scan2-indels|Luquette 2022]]; [[10-Summaries/bizzotto-2022-brain-mosaicism-review|Bizzotto 2022]]).

**Clonal expansion and hypermutation**

- Cell-type-specific clonal expansion in aged hematopoiesis ([[10-Summaries/kapadia-2024-stem-cell-aging|Kapadia 2024]]).
- ~6% of brains are hypermutable (>101 somatic SNVs), rising to 16% of brains over 60, possibly precursor states for glioma ([[10-Summaries/taejeong-2022-science|Bae 2022]]).

## Open questions

- Is genosenium *causal* for aging-related disease, or a *biomarker*? Distinction has therapeutic implications.
- The two neuronal SNV rates for prefrontal cortex (~23/year by MDA, ~16.5/year by PTA) come from different amplification chemistries ([[10-Summaries/lodato-2017-aging-neurons|Lodato 2018]]; [[10-Summaries/luquette-2022-neuron-scan2-indels|Luquette 2022]]).
