# SomaGauss-SV

> A somatic structural-variant detection workflow for paired tumor-vs-normal nanopore long-read data (Liu et al. 2025). Balances precision and recall across SV types (deletions, insertions, duplications, inversions, translocations).

## Definition

Designed for high-sensitivity detection of cancer-driven structural variation. Benchmarked against five paired tumor cell-line datasets. Applied to 15 paired laryngeal SCC tumor-blood samples to discover deletion-burden ↔ smoking intensity correlation and a high-frequency repeat-expansion.
