# Alexey V. Onufriev

> Professor in the Departments of Computer Science and Physics at **Virginia Tech**, affiliated with the Center for Soft Matter and Biological Physics. Specialty: biophysical / polymer-physics modeling of chromatin in 3D, with emphasis on the relationship between lamina–chromatin interactions and nuclear architecture.

## Selected work

- **Tolokh, Onufriev et al. 2023** — Langevin dynamics models of *Drosophila* interphase nuclei combining bulk Hi-C and lamina-DamID restraints; ~11-hour biological trajectories of 18 diploid nuclei.
- **Mali, Onufriev et al. 2025** ([[10-Summaries/mali-2025-conformational-heterogeneity]]) — Conformational Heterogeneity (C.H.) metric for cell-to-cell 3D variability; comparison of bulk- vs scHi-C-trained models; lamin-depletion prediction.

## Collaborators

- Sergey Ulianov, Ekaterina Khrameeva — providers of scHi-C *Drosophila* data.
