# Thierry Voet

> KU Leuven human geneticist; co-developer of [[gt-seq|G&T-seq]] and senior author of [[10-Summaries/katy-2023-naturereviewsgenetics]]. Founding director of the KU Leuven Institute for Single Cell Omics (LISCO).

## Profile

KU Leuven Institute for Single Cell Omics (LISCO); Department of Human Genetics, University of Leuven. Email thierry.voet@kuleuven.be.

## Mentions

- **2026-05-11** — [[10-Summaries/katy-2023-naturereviewsgenetics]]: senior author of the single-cell and spatial multi-omics review.

## Positions and claims

- Holds that **multi-omic methods are best organized by *when* analytes are uncoupled** — before, during, or after library preparation — rather than purely by modality pair ([[10-Summaries/katy-2023-naturereviewsgenetics]]).
- Holds that **direct nuclear DNA tagmentation (DNTR-seq)** can circumvent WGA artifacts at the cost of lower coverage — a genuine architectural alternative for some applications.
