# Pavel A. Pevzner

> UC San Diego. Introduced de Bruijn graphs to fragment assembly and drove the algorithmic response to single-cell amplification bias.

## Mentions

- **2026-08-13** — Coauthor of [[chitsaz-2011-velvet-sc]] (E+V-SC), the first assembler adapted to MDA's coverage catastrophe via a progressively increasing coverage cutoff. That paper is also where the field's framing shifts: *"the challenges facing single-cell genomics are increasingly computational rather than experimental."*
- **2026-08-13** — Corresponding author of [[bankevich-2012-spades]] (SPAdes), which rebuilt assembly around the **paired de Bruijn graph** made practical by *k*-bimer adjustment. Two SPAdes authors had coauthored E+V-SC the year before and stated plainly why they returned: *"one needs to change algorithmic design (rather than just modify existing tools) to fully utilize the potential of SCS."*
- Of the three 2011–2012 single-cell assemblers, only SPAdes remained in routine use — the tool that reformulated the problem outlived the tools that patched it. (synthesis)
