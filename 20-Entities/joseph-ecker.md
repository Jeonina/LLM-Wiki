# Joseph Ecker

> PI at Salk Institute; pioneer of whole-genome bisulfite sequencing in plants and single-cell methylome profiling. Developed snmC-seq2 and led the BICCN single-cell methylation atlas of the mouse brain.

## Profile

- Affiliation: Salk Institute for Biological Studies; HHMI investigator
- Research focus: DNA methylation, single-cell methylome profiling, neuronal methylation diversity, BICCN

## Added 2026-08-13

Corresponding author of [[10-Summaries/luo-2017-snmc-seq]], the founding snmC-seq paper. Its enabling insight is that **mCH is modulated over large domains**, so 100-kb-bin estimates are accurate across >90% of the genome despite only 4.7–5.7% coverage per cell — which is what makes methylation a primary cell-typing modality. 16 mouse and 21 human cortical neuron clusters; ~500,000 CG-DMRs per species, ~70% of them >10 kb from any TSS.
