# Xiaocheng Weng

> Wuhan University chemical biologist (PhD Wuhan U, 2007). Develops chemical-biology methods for nucleic-acid epigenetic modifications, nucleic-acid structures, and interactions between nucleic acids and proteins. Active in single-cell multiomics review/methodology.
