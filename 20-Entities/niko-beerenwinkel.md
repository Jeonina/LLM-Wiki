# Niko Beerenwinkel

> Computational biologist leading the group behind SCITE, SCIΦ and SCICoNE, a line of probabilistic tree-based methods for single-cell tumour phylogenetics ([[10-Summaries/jahn-2016-scite]]; [[10-Summaries/singer-2018-sciphi]]; [[10-Summaries/kuipers-2025-scicone]]).

## Profile

The group's methods share one approach: infer the evolutionary tree jointly with the noisy single-cell genotypes, so the phylogeny denoises the calls (synthesis). The lineage runs from SNV mutation trees (SCITE) through phylogeny-aided mutation calling (SCIΦ) to copy-number event trees that drop the infinite-sites assumption (SCICoNE) ([[10-Summaries/jahn-2016-scite]]; [[10-Summaries/singer-2018-sciphi]]; [[10-Summaries/kuipers-2025-scicone]]).

## Mentions

- **2026-10-07** — senior author (supervision and funding lead) of [[10-Summaries/kuipers-2025-scicone]] (SCICoNE: joint single-cell CN calling and CNA tree inference).
- **2026-10-07** — Co-author of [[10-Summaries/sollier-2023-compass]] (COMPASS: joint SNV + CNA phylogenies from amplicon scDNA-seq).
- **2026-10-07** — co-author of [[10-Summaries/kang-2022-sieve]] (SIEVE: finite-sites joint inference of SNVs and cell phylogeny with acquisition-bias correction).
