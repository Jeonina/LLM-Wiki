# Rahul Satija

> NYGC / NYU computational biologist; developer of the **Seurat** single-cell analysis toolkit and a major figure in multi-omic integration methods. Co-senior author of [[10-Summaries/baysoy-2023-multiomics-landscape]].

## Profile

New York Genome Center; Center for Genomics and Systems Biology, New York University.

## Mentions

- **2026-05-11** — [[10-Summaries/baysoy-2023-multiomics-landscape]]: co-senior author of the multi-omics review.

## Positions and claims

- Holds that **Seurat's Weighted Nearest Neighbor (WNN) integration** is a robust approach for vertical multi-omic integration (same cells, different modalities) ([[10-Summaries/baysoy-2023-multiomics-landscape]]).

## Added 2026-08-17

Corresponding author of two of the three Seurat integration papers ingested 2026-08-14: [[10-Summaries/butler-2018-seurat-cca]] (CCA manifold alignment, 2018) and [[10-Summaries/hao-2021-seurat-wnn]] (weighted nearest neighbor, Seurat v4, 2021).

The through-line is a single continuous programme: **CCA alignment (2018) → anchors (v3) → WNN (v4, 2021) → [[10-Summaries/hao-2024-seurat-v5|bridge integration]] (v5, 2024)**, with the chromatin arm at [[10-Summaries/stuart-2021-natmethods]] (Signac). WNN's contribution is per-cell modality weighting — learning the relative utility of each data type *in each cell* — validated on a 211,000-cell CITE-seq PBMC atlas with 228 antibodies. (synthesis)
