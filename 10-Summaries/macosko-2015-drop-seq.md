**Citation:** Macosko et al. (2015) — *Drop-seq: highly parallel scRNA-seq via nanoliter droplets* — *Cell*. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2015.05.002)

Macosko, Basu, Satija et al. (McCarroll, Regev, Shalek labs; Broad/Harvard) introduced **Drop-seq** ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2015.05.002)), a droplet-microfluidic method that co-encapsulates single cells with barcoded microparticles in ~1-nL aqueous droplets, enabling parallel scRNA-seq of thousands of cells per run at **~6.5 cents per cell** — a >100× cost and time reduction over plate-based methods. Each bead carries oligo-dT primers concatenated with (i) a constant PCR/sequencing site, (ii) a cell barcode (12 nt, 1 of ~17M sequences generated by 12 split-and-pool synthesis cycles), (iii) a UMI (8 nt random), and (iv) the oligo-dT capture tail. After droplet lysis, cell mRNAs hybridize to their companion bead forming a **STAMP (Single-cell Transcriptomes Attached to Microparticles)**; STAMPs are broken out, reverse-transcribed in bulk, and 3'-end sequenced. Validation on human-mouse cell mixtures gave species-mixing rates of 0.36–11.3% (concentration-dependent), single-cell purity 90–99%, and a transcript capture rate of **~12.8%** corroborated by ddPCR (10.7%). Applied to mouse retina (P14), the authors profiled **44,808 cells** and recovered **39 transcriptionally distinct populations**, matching all five known neuronal classes plus glia/vasculature and revealing fine-grained amacrine and ipRGC subtypes including new markers (e.g. PPP1R17 for nGnG amacrines).

## Why this matters

**The scaling breakthrough that made cell atlases possible.** Before Drop-seq (and inDrop, published the same Cell issue by Klein et al.), scRNA-seq throughput was a few hundred to a few thousand cells per study using FACS sorting or the Fluidigm C1 chip. Drop-seq dropped per-cell cost two orders of magnitude and made tens-of-thousands-of-cells experiments routine, directly enabling the Human Cell Atlas era and the entire 10x Genomics commercial platform (which is conceptually a refined Drop-seq). For a multi-omics review, Drop-seq is the foundation under every droplet-based multi-omics derivative: [[10-Summaries/nam-2019-got|GoT (Nam 2019)]] grafts targeted genotyping onto the 10x/Drop-seq backbone, multiome (10x ATAC+RNA), DOGMA-seq (see [[30-Concepts/dogma-seq]]), and Tapestri DAb-seq all inherit the bead-barcode + droplet architecture. The cell-barcode-via-split-pool-synthesis trick also seeds [[30-Concepts/combinatorial-indexing|combinatorial-indexing]] approaches.

## Key claims and evidence

- **Throughput**: 10,000 single-cell libraries per 12 hours of bench work; cost ~$0.065 per cell.
- **Capture efficiency**: 12.8% transcript capture (UMI-collapsed, conservative estimate) — confirmed independently by ddPCR at 10.7% on the same bulk RNA.
- **Single-cell purity**: 98.8% at 12.5 cells/µL, 90.4% at 100 cells/µL (concentration-purity tradeoff documented).
- **Doublet rate**: 0.36–11.3% across cell concentrations, comparable or better than Fluidigm C1 (≈30% species-mixed in their hands).
- **Retina atlas**: 44,808 cells → 39 clusters spanning all 5 neuronal classes, glia, vasculature, with **rare cell type detection** (clusters as small as 0.1–0.9% of input) emerging as cell numbers grew.
- **Replicability**: 7 retina runs across 4 days yielded all 39 clusters in every replicate.

## Method components (relevance for review writing)

| Component | Role | Successor uses |
|---|---|---|
| Split-pool bead synthesis | Generate 17M unique cell barcodes | 10x Chromium gel beads, inDrop, SPLiT-seq |
| Oligo-dT capture | Poly-A selection | All 3'-end scRNA-seq |
| UMI on each primer | PCR duplicate collapse | Universal in modern scRNA-seq (see [[30-Concepts/umi-molecular-barcoding]]) |
| STAMP intermediate | Bead-bound cDNA stable between droplet and pooled RT | inDrop, 10x, Tapestri all variants |
| Species-mixing QC | Quantitative doublet/purity measurement | Standard validation across droplet methods |

## Limitations (as relevant for review writing)

- **3'-end bias**: Drop-seq only sequences transcript 3' ends → no isoform-level resolution. Full-length protocols (Smart-seq2/3) trade throughput for transcript coverage.
- **Gene dropout**: 12.8% capture rate means low-expressed genes are systematically zero-inflated — a defining limitation of droplet scRNA-seq quantified rigorously by [[10-Summaries/svensson-2017-power-analysis|Svensson 2017]].
- **Cell-suspension requirement**: tissue must be dissociated; spatial information is lost (motivating spatial transcriptomics).
- **No DNA/chromatin/protein**: pure transcriptomics — motivates joint-omics extensions ([[10-Summaries/dey-2015-dr-seq|DR-seq]], [[10-Summaries/hou-2016-sctrio-seq|scTrio-seq]], CITE-seq).
