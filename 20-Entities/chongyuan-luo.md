# Chongyuan Luo

> Developed snmC-seq in the Ecker lab; now runs a single-cell epigenomics lab at UCLA. His work established DNA methylation as a primary cell-typing modality rather than a supplementary readout.

## Mentions

- **2026-08-13** — First author of [[luo-2017-snmc-seq]], which used non-CG methylation (mCH) in 100-kb bins to type 16 mouse and 21 human cortical neuron clusters from >6,000 single methylomes, and called ~500,000 CG-DMRs per species as regulatory elements.
- **2026-08-13** — First author of [[luo-2018-snmc-seq2]], the throughput and robustness successor.

## Why the mCH insight mattered

Coverage per cell is only 4.7–5.7% of the genome, but mCH is modulated over large domains, so 100-kb-bin estimates hold across >90% of the genome ([[luo-2017-snmc-seq]]). The cell-type labels produced this way are now the annotation substrate for the single-cell 3D genome field — [[yu-2021-snaphic]], [[xiong-2024-scghost]] and [[park-2026-mintsc]] all label prefrontal cortex cells by methylation, independently of chromatin contacts.
