Bizzotto and Walsh (Boston Children's/Harvard) reviewed brain genetic mosaicism in Nature Reviews Neuroscience. Sections: mechanisms of brain GM (developmental SNV accumulation rates: ~2-3 per division early, ~5 per day in fetal neural progenitors, ~16-20/year in adult postmitotic neurons; CpG-deamination signature pre-gastrulation → oxidative-damage in neurogenesis → clock-like signature in aging neurons), lineage tracing from natural somatic mutations (asymmetric 50:50 to 90:10 contributions from first 2 postzygotic cells), cortical development clonal architecture (PRDD-seq, ~50-100 forebrain founder progenitors, intermingling clones), neuropsychiatric disease (epileptic malformations, ASD).
