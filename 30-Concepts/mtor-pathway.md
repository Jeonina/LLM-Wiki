# mTOR pathway

> A central signaling pathway controlling cell growth, proliferation, and protein synthesis. Activated by growth factors (via PI3K → AKT), nutrients, and energy status. Somatic mutations that hyperactivate the pathway in neural progenitors cause focal cortical dysplasia and hemimegalencephaly ([[10-Summaries/bizzotto-2022-brain-mosaicism-review|Bizzotto 2022]]).

> [!abstract] 한눈에
> mTOR pathway는 세포 성장, 증식, 단백질 합성을 조절하는 중심 신호 경로다. 신경 전구세포에서 이 경로를 과활성화하는 체세포 변이는 활성화 유전자의 gain-of-function이든 억제 유전자의 loss-of-function이든 focal cortical dysplasia와 hemimegalencephaly를 일으킨다 ([[10-Summaries/bizzotto-2022-brain-mosaicism-review|Bizzotto 2022]]).

## Definition

mTOR exists in two complexes — TORC1 (rapamycin-sensitive, growth/translation regulator) and TORC2 (rapamycin-resistant, cytoskeletal/AKT regulator). The pathway is normally restrained by tumor-suppressor proteins TSC1/TSC2 (Tuberous Sclerosis Complex), DEPDC5, NPRL2, NPRL3, PTEN.

## Why it matters

- **Cancer**: hyperactive mTOR drives tumor growth across many cancer types.
- **Neurodevelopment**: somatic gain-of-function in *AKT3, PIK3CA, RHEB, MTOR* or loss-of-function in repressors *TSC1, TSC2, DEPDC5, PTEN, NPRL2/3* causes focal cortical dysplasia / hemimegalencephaly ([[30-Concepts/focal-cortical-dysplasia]]; [[10-Summaries/bizzotto-2022-brain-mosaicism-review|Bizzotto 2022]]).
- **Therapeutic target**: mTOR inhibitors (rapamycin/sirolimus, everolimus, temsirolimus) treat tuberous sclerosis complex and are under trial for FCD.

## Examples

- FCD2 / HME mutations span the pathway both ways (activators and repressors) ([[10-Summaries/bizzotto-2022-brain-mosaicism-review|Bizzotto 2022]]).
