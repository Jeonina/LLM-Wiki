# Post-zygotic variation

> Genetic changes acquired after fertilization, not present in the zygote and not transmitted constitutionally through the germline. The unifying term used by [[10-Summaries/forsberg-2017-mosaicism-review]] to encompass all somatic and germline mosaic mutations.

## Definition

Any DNA change — SNV, indel, CNV, structural variant, aneuploidy — that arises in a cell of an individual after fertilization. The mutation may be present in some subset of cells (somatic mosaicism) or in some subset of germ cells (gonadal mosaicism), or both (gonosomal mosaicism).

Distinguishes from:

- **Constitutional variants** — present in every cell of the individual (germline-transmitted).
- **De novo germline variants** — present in every cell because they arose in a parental gamete or the zygote.
- **Chimerism** — multiple genotypes from multiple fertilization events.
- **[[microchimerism]]** — small populations of cells from a different individual.

## Why it matters

Post-zygotic variation is the molecular substrate of [[40-Topics/somatic-mosaicism]] and the focus of single-cell DNA sequencing for human-development and disease applications. The framing is useful clinically because it predicts:

- Whether a mutation may be transmitted to offspring (depends on whether it predates PGC differentiation — [[developmental-mutation-timing]]).
- Whether bulk DNA from one tissue captures the mutation (depends on which lineages inherit it).

## Variants and refinements

- **Single-base** (SNV) vs **structural** (indel, CNV, SV) — different detection requirements.
- **Mosaic VAF** — variant allele frequency depends on when and where the mutation arose; ranges from <1% (late, single lineage) to ~50% (first mitosis).

## Contested points

- Whether the term should include normal-aging mutational background (every cell has them) or be restricted to clinically meaningful variants — definitional, not factual.

## Examples

- Mosaic CALR or JAK2 mutations in MPN clonal hematopoiesis ([[10-Summaries/izzo-2024-got-cha]]).
- CHILD syndrome (first-mitosis post-zygotic mutation) — half-body phenotype.
