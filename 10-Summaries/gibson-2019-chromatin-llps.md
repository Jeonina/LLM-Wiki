# Gibson et al. 2019 — Chromatin LLPS

> Thesis: reconstituted chromatin undergoes **histone-tail-driven liquid-liquid phase separation (LLPS)** at physiologic salt — both in vitro and when microinjected into cell nuclei. Linker histone H1 and inter-nucleosome linker length tune droplet density. Histone acetylation by p300 dissolves the droplets; multi-bromodomain readers (BRD4) bind hyperacetylated chromatin to form a distinct, *immiscible* phase. The chromatin polymer's intrinsic capacity for LLPS provides a physical framework for understanding how distinct chromatin compartments are established and maintained.

## Verbatim key claims (from source body)

- **Definition of intrinsic chromatin LLPS** (Introduction, final paragraph):
  > "physiological concentrations of cations induce reversible LLPS of reconstituted nucleosomal arrays (chromatin hereafter), producing **dynamic histone tail-dependent droplets with nucleosome concentrations similar to those estimated for cells**."

- **Role of linker histone H1**:
  > "The linker **histone H1 promotes phase separation, increasing the concentration of nucleosomes within droplets and decreasing droplet dynamics**, consistent with the role of this protein as a repressive chromatin architectural factor that promotes chromatin condensation in cells."

- **Nucleosome spacing rule**:
  > "The spacing of nucleosomes every **10n+5 bp, which is predominant to 10n spacing in cells, strongly favors phase separation of chromatin**, further suggesting that cells use this nucleosome-driven phase separation in regulation and organization of the genome."

- **Linker-length × H1 interaction**:
  > "Histone H1 is more effective at concentrating chromatin with longer linkers, whereas short linkers produce high-concentration condensates in the absence of linker histone."

- **Acetylation dissolves droplets**:
  > "Histone acetylation causes dissolution of chromatin droplets. **Microinjected chromatin forms droplets in the nucleus of a cell that are largely abrogated by chromatin acetylation prior to injection**."

- **BRD4 creates a new phase**:
  > "Highly acetylated chromatin can be induced to re-phase-separate by multi-bromodomain proteins, including the transcriptional regulator BRD4. **Droplets formed by acetylated chromatin plus a penta-bromodomain protein associate, but do not coalesce, with droplets of non-acetylated chromatin.**"

- **Functional consequence — immiscible chromatin compartments** (last sentence of Intro):
  > "These observations illustrate how **LLPS could produce functionally distinct but physically adjacent chromatin regions in vivo** and suggest that the intrinsic capacity of the chromatin polymer to undergo LLPS may play important roles in organization and regulation of eukaryotic genomes."

- **Highlights (paper top)**:
  > • Chromatin undergoes liquid-liquid phase separation (LLPS) under physiologic conditions
  > • Linker DNA length and patterning, histone H1, and acetylation modulate chromatin LLPS
  > • Acetylated chromatin only phase separates upon binding multi-bromodomain proteins
  > • LLPS could enable establishment and maintenance of distinct chromatin compartments

## Why this matters for the wiki

- **Foundational anchor** for the biophysical / mechanical sub-axis of DNA locus state. The verbatim quotes above directly support "rigidity, condensation, elasticity, and fluid-like behavior" framing in the user's locus-state framework.
- **Bridges epigenetic and structural axes**: acetylation (epigenetic mark) flips chromatin between distinct phase states (structural state) via reader-protein recruitment. This is mechanism-level coupling between two of the three locus-state axes.
- **Connects to oncogenic LLPS** ([[10-Summaries/ahn-2021-llps-cancer-looping]]) — NUP98-HOXA9 IDR fusions hijack the same physical machinery to drive aberrant 3D architecture.
- Frames downstream **viscoelastic / mechanical measurements** ([[10-Summaries/daugird-2024-viscoelastic-chromatin]]) and **polymer-network modeling** ([[10-Summaries/qi-zhang-2021-nucleoli-coalescence]]).

## Methods / evidence (from text)

- Reconstituted nucleosomal arrays from purified *X. laevis* histone octamers
- Physiological salt droplet assays; FRAP for liquid character; in vitro biochemistry by Gibson + Doolittle
- **Nuclear microinjection of fluorescently labeled chromatin** (performed by Schneider/Gerlich) — in-vivo confirmation
- BRD4 penta-bromodomain (synthetic) for re-phase-separation experiments

## Open questions

- Quantitative mapping of in-vitro LLPS phase diagrams onto endogenous chromatin where ATP, RNA, and many regulators compete with H1.
- **Genome-wide boundaries between immiscible phases** — do they align with TAD boundaries, A/B compartments, or LAD edges?
- Therapeutic targeting of LLPS without dissolving essential physiologic condensates (1,6-hexanediol toxicity).
