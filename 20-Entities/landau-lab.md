# Landau Lab

> Research group led by [[20-Entities/dan-a-landau]] at the New York Genome Center / Weill Cornell Medicine; develops methods for joint single-cell measurement of somatic genotype with phenotype (RNA, chromatin, surface protein), and applies them to clonal evolution in hematological malignancies.

## Profile

Hosted at the New York Genome Center and the Division of Hematology and Medical Oncology, Weill Cornell Medicine. The methods program produces a sequence of joint genotype + phenotype single-cell technologies on droplet platforms (10x Genomics):

- 2019: [[30-Concepts/got]] (genotype + scRNA-seq)
- 2019: [[30-Concepts/circularization-got]] (extension for distal loci)
- 2024: [[30-Concepts/got-cha]] (genotype + scATAC-seq via gDNA capture)
- 2024 (same paper): GoT–ChA + [[30-Concepts/dogma-seq]] integration for genotype + chromatin + RNA + surface protein

## Mentions

- **2026-05-07** — [[10-Summaries/anna-2019-nature]]: established GoT.
- **2026-05-07** — [[10-Summaries/franco-2024-nature]]: established GoT–ChA, extending the platform from RNA to chromatin and from cDNA to gDNA capture.

## Positions and claims

- Holds that **co-capture of genotype with phenotype in the same single cell** is the right unit of analysis for clonal evolution in human samples — within-patient WT comparators control confounders that no cross-patient design can ([[10-Summaries/anna-2019-nature]], [[10-Summaries/franco-2024-nature]]).
- Holds that **gDNA capture beats cDNA capture for single-cell genotyping** of low-expression and distal-locus drivers — a methodological lesson learned across the GoT → GoT–ChA evolution ([[10-Summaries/franco-2024-nature]]).
