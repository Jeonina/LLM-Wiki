# Autism spectrum disorder

> A heterogeneous neurodevelopmental disorder with both germline and somatic genetic contributions. Recent work finds mosaic missense mutations in intolerant genes in 0.8–1.3% of probands and large (>4 Mb) mosaic CNVs in 0.2% ([[10-Summaries/bizzotto-2022-brain-mosaicism-review|Bizzotto 2022]]).

> [!abstract] 한눈에
> ASD는 germline 변이와 somatic mosaic 변이가 함께 기여하는 이질적인 신경발달 장애다. proband의 0.8–1.3%가 intolerant gene의 mosaic missense 변이를, 0.2%가 4 Mb 이상의 mosaic CNV를 가진다 ([[10-Summaries/bizzotto-2022-brain-mosaicism-review|Bizzotto 2022]]).

## Definition

ASD is defined by clinical criteria (social communication deficits, restricted/repetitive behaviors). Genetically heterogeneous: common GWAS variants, rare de novo germline variants (CNVs, SNVs, tandem repeats), and **somatic mosaic mutations** all contribute.

## Why it matters

ASD risk is not captured entirely by inherited variation ([[10-Summaries/bizzotto-2022-brain-mosaicism-review|Bizzotto 2022]]). Deep WGS of ASD brains shows excess mosaic mutations in brain-active enhancers — a finding that exome sequencing misses ([[10-Summaries/bizzotto-2022-brain-mosaicism-review|Bizzotto 2022]]).

## Evidence and examples

**Somatic mosaic variants**

- Mosaic missense mutations in intolerant genes occur in 0.8–1.3% of ASD probands ([[10-Summaries/bizzotto-2022-brain-mosaicism-review|Bizzotto 2022]]).
- Large mosaic CNVs (>4 Mb) occur in 0.2% of probands ([[10-Summaries/bizzotto-2022-brain-mosaicism-review|Bizzotto 2022]]) and correlate positively with ASD severity.

**Germline de novo variants**

- Germline de novo mutations in six genes (CHD8, DYRK1A, GRIN2B, TBR1, PTEN, TBL1XR1) were found in ~1% (24/2,573) of Simons Simplex Collection probands, CHD8 alone at 0.35% ([[10-Summaries/oroak-2012-autism-targeted-seq|O'Roak 2012]]).
- CHD8 mutations go with larger and DYRK1A with smaller heads ([[10-Summaries/oroak-2012-autism-targeted-seq|O'Roak 2012]]).

## Open questions

- What fraction of risk comes from intronic/intergenic somatic variants invisible to exome sequencing? The enhancer-excess finding is limited by cohort size ([[10-Summaries/bizzotto-2022-brain-mosaicism-review|Bizzotto 2022]]).
