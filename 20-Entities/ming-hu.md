# Ming Hu

> Cleveland Clinic Lerner Research Institute. Statistical methodology for chromatin interaction data (HMRF/Fit-Hi-C lineage, MAPS, SnapHiC).

## Mentions

- **2026-08-13** — Corresponding author of [[yu-2021-snaphic]], the first chromatin-loop caller designed for [[single-cell-hi-c|scHi-C]] rather than borrowed from bulk. Its design decision — treat each cell as an independent sample and run a paired *t*-test across cells, rather than pooling into pseudobulk — turns cell-to-cell variance into statistical power and calls 1,050–1,420 loops from 75 cells where HiCCUPS calls 0–10.
