# Fuchou Tang

> First author of the founding scRNA-seq paper ([[10-Summaries/tang-2009-scrna-seq|Tang 2009]]); pioneer of single-cell genomics. Trained as a postdoc in M. Azim Surani's lab at the Gurdon Institute, University of Cambridge, and subsequently PI at Peking University BIOPIC / School of Life Sciences, where his group has continued to develop single-cell multi-omics methods (including STEM-seq, scTrio-seq for hepatocellular carcinoma, and scCOOL-seq).

## Profile

Affiliations: Wellcome Trust-Cancer Research UK Gurdon Institute, University of Cambridge (postdoc, c. 2009); Biomedical Pioneering Innovation Center (BIOPIC) and School of Life Sciences, Peking University (PI, current). The 2009 Nature Methods paper from the Surani lab marked the conceptual birth of single-cell transcriptomics by sequencing — a category that subsequent droplet, plate, and combinatorial-indexing methods all extend.

## Mentions

- **2026-05-14** — [[10-Summaries/tang-2009-scrna-seq]]: first author. Demonstrated mRNA-Seq from a single mouse blastomere, detecting 75% more genes than microarray and 1,753 novel splice junctions.
- **2026-05-14** — [[10-Summaries/hou-2016-sctrio-seq]]: senior/corresponding author on the scTrio-seq triple-omics paper, extending single-cell sequencing to joint genome + methylome + transcriptome in hepatocellular carcinoma cells.

## Positions and claims

- Single-cell mRNA-Seq from a single blastomere is feasible and reveals transcriptional complexity invisible to microarray analysis ([[10-Summaries/tang-2009-scrna-seq]]).
- Single-cell transcriptomics can be coupled with single-cell genome and methylome readouts in a single workflow ([[10-Summaries/hou-2016-sctrio-seq]]).
