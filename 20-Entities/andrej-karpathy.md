# Andrej Karpathy

> Researcher and educator working on neural networks and large language models; proposed the "LLM Wiki" pattern of using LLMs to maintain persistent, compounding markdown knowledge bases.

## Profile

Background in deep learning research and ML education. Known publicly for accessible writing and teaching about neural networks and language models. Appears in this wiki as the originator of the LLM-as-maintainer wiki pattern this vault implements.

## Mentions

- **2026-05-07** — [[10-Summaries/example-llm-wiki]] paraphrases his proposal of the [[30-Concepts/llm-wiki]] pattern: a [[30-Concepts/three-layer-architecture]] (sources / wiki / schema) where the LLM does the cross-reference work humans abandon.

## Positions and claims

- Holds that LLM-maintained wikis can be a [[30-Concepts/compounding-artifact]] because the LLM happily makes the 10–15 cross-reference edits per ingest that humans batch and defer ([[10-Summaries/example-llm-wiki]]).
- Holds that flat markdown + `index.md` + `log.md` is sufficient navigation infrastructure — no embedding-based retrieval required ([[10-Summaries/example-llm-wiki]]).
