# Jan P. Dumanski

> Uppsala-based somatic-mosaicism geneticist; corresponding author on the 2017 NRG review establishing the structural-variant-centric framing of post-zygotic variation (introducing ACE terminology, LOY as the most common human post-zygotic mutation). Long-time collaborator with [[20-Entities/lars-forsberg]] on the Uppsala LOY-cohort studies.

## Mentions in this wiki

- **2016-12-12** — Corresponding author on [[10-Summaries/forsberg-2017-mosaicism-clones]], the NRG review framing structural variants (CNV/LOY/CNNLOH) as the dominant ACE category in human aging.
