# Myeloproliferative neoplasm (MPN)

> Group of clonal hematological diseases — essential thrombocythemia (ET), polycythemia vera (PV), and primary myelofibrosis (MF) — driven by somatic mutations in JAK2, CALR, or MPL that cause overproduction of mature myeloid lineages and progressive marrow dysfunction.

## Definition

MPNs originate in [[hematopoietic-differentiation|hematopoietic stem cells]] and are diagnosed by lineage-specific cytoses (platelets in ET, red cells in PV, fibrosis with cytopenia in MF) plus a driver mutation. The three canonical drivers ([[jak2-v617f]], [[calr-mutation]], MPL W515) are largely mutually exclusive and converge on JAK-STAT activation.

A subset of patients evolve PV → MF or ET → MF; a smaller fraction transform to acute myeloid leukemia.

## Why it matters

MPN is a tractable model for studying **how a single somatic mutation perturbs human hematopoiesis** because:

- The mutations are stable, well-characterized, and present in a substantial mutated-cell fraction;
- Bone marrow CD34⁺ samples are clinically obtainable;
- Mutated and wild-type cells coexist in the same patient — the **internal-control design** that makes [[got]] and [[got-cha]] uniquely powerful here.

Together [[10-Summaries/anna-2019-nature]] and [[10-Summaries/franco-2024-nature]] established that:

- The same mutation produces **different transcriptional outputs in different progenitor cell types** ([[10-Summaries/anna-2019-nature]]).
- The same mutation produces **cell-intrinsic chromatin priming** that is visible *before* overt disease ([[10-Summaries/franco-2024-nature]]).

## Variants and refinements

- **Essential thrombocythemia (ET)** — elevated platelets; CALR-mutant fitness advantage grows with myeloid differentiation ([[10-Summaries/anna-2019-nature]]).
- **Polycythemia vera (PV)** — elevated red cells; mostly JAK2V617F.
- **Primary myelofibrosis (MF)** — bone-marrow fibrosis, cytopenias; mutant cells already enriched in HSPCs ([[10-Summaries/anna-2019-nature]]); MkPs upregulate TGFB1.
- **JAK2V617F clonal hematopoiesis** — pre-disease state; chromatin priming already detectable ([[10-Summaries/franco-2024-nature]]).
- **Triple mutants** — co-occurring CALR + NFE2 + SF3B1 dissectable via multiplexed [[got]] ([[10-Summaries/anna-2019-nature]]).

## Contested points

- Why CALR fitness advantage is differentiation-dependent in ET but already strong at the HSPC level in MF is observed but unexplained ([[10-Summaries/anna-2019-nature]]).
- Whether ruxolitinib's failure to eliminate the JAK2V617F clone — despite reversing the chromatin TF-motif phenotype — argues for combination therapy targeting the cell-intrinsic chromatin program directly ([[10-Summaries/franco-2024-nature]]).

## Examples

- ET01–ET05 (5 patients), MF01–MF04, MF05 multi-mutant — [[10-Summaries/anna-2019-nature]] cohort.
- 18 untreated/ruxolitinib-treated MF patients + 1 longitudinal PV→MF + 1 JAK2V617F clonal hematopoiesis case — [[10-Summaries/franco-2024-nature]] cohort.
