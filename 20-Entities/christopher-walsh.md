# Christopher Walsh

> Pediatric neurologist and HHMI investigator at Boston Children's / Harvard; long-running PI of the most prominent human-brain somatic mosaicism research program. Senior author on [[10-Summaries/shao-2025-scDNA-mosaicism-review]] and on foundational papers establishing that human cortical neurons carry mosaic mutations as endogenous lineage markers.

## Profile

Boston Children's Hospital, Harvard Medical School, Howard Hughes Medical Institute. Email Christopher.Walsh@childrens.harvard.edu. The lab has been at the forefront of human brain genome biology, including:

- The 2015 Lodato et al. *Science* paper demonstrating that single human cortical neurons accumulate hundreds of somatic SNVs detectable by MDA scWGS.
- Subsequent work using PTA to track neuronal lineage and identify the dorsal source of cortical inhibitory neurons.
- The 2026 preprint showing 40% of mid-gestation human prenatal neurons harbor complex CNV.

## Mentions

- **2026-05-11** — [[10-Summaries/shao-2025-scDNA-mosaicism-review]]: senior author of the keystone scDNA-seq review. Frames the current state of the field through the lens of mosaicism research that his lab has been driving for over a decade.
- **2026-05-12** — [[10-Summaries/bizzotto-2022-brain-mosaicism-review]]: senior author of the NRN brain mosaicism review with first-author postdoc Sara Bizzotto. Articulates the framework that "somatic developmental mutations are natural barcodes for retrospective lineage tracing in humans."
- **2026-05-12** — Co-author of [[10-Summaries/luquette-2025-pta-duplex-mosaicism]] (Luquette/Walsh 2025): PTA + duplex on 102 nuclei from 74-yo lung and colon; SMaHT-network single-cell paper.
- **2026-05-12** — Co-author of [[10-Summaries/zhang-2025-smaht-duplex-benchmark]] (SMaHT duplex-seq benchmark).

## Positions and claims

- Holds that **human brain somatic mosaicism is much more pervasive than previously appreciated** — neuronal progenitors accumulate ~25 SNVs per cell per week during mid-gestation, postnatal neurons ~15 per year ([[10-Summaries/shao-2025-scDNA-mosaicism-review]]).
- Holds that **lineage tracing in human brain via endogenous somatic mutations** is technically feasible with current scDNA-seq (PTA + scWGS) and is producing fundamental insights into brain development ([[10-Summaries/shao-2025-scDNA-mosaicism-review]]).
