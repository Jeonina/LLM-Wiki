# Jay A.A. West

> Single-cell genomics technologist; corresponding author on the ResolveOME paper and a founder/scientific lead associated with BioSkryb Genomics, which commercializes PTA-based whole-genome and ResolveOME whole-genome+transcriptome single-cell kits.

## Profile

Affiliation: BioSkryb Genomics. Work centers on primary template-directed amplification ([[pta]]) and its productization for accurate single-cell whole-genome and joint genome+transcriptome assays, paired with the BaseJumper bioinformatics platform.

## Mentions

- **2026-06-26** — [[10-Summaries/marks-2023-resolveome]]: corresponding author. Unified PTA whole-genome amplification with full-transcript RNA-seq in the same cell (ResolveOME) and applied it to AML drug resistance and primary breast cancer.

## Positions and claims

- Holds that **genome-wide single-cell genotype is only fully interpretable alongside the same cell's transcriptome**, which supplies cell identity and state ([[10-Summaries/marks-2023-resolveome]]).
- Holds that **PTA's low-error, uniform whole-genome amplification** is the right foundation for joint DNA+RNA single-cell assays ([[10-Summaries/marks-2023-resolveome]]).
