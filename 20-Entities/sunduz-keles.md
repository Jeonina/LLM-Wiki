# Sündüz Keleş

> University of Wisconsin–Madison, biostatistics. Statistical models for high-dimensional and sparse genomic data.

## Mentions

- **2026-08-13** — Corresponding author of [[park-2026-mintsc]] (MINTsC), which reframes [[single-cell-hi-c|scHi-C]] as a multilayer network so that a [[multi-way-chromatin-interaction|multi-way interaction]] becomes a clique. The statistical core is a Dirichlet-multinomial model with a natural cubic spline over genomic-distance bands, plus a clique statistic built from order statistics of pairwise p-values with an analytic Beta null.
- The paper's most transferable application is statistical rather than structural: multi-way interactions supply a prior that collapses the multiple-testing burden for **epistatic SNP effects** in eQTL analysis — demonstrated on ROS/MAP Alzheimer's cortex data for *DKK3* and *CPLX2* ([[park-2026-mintsc]]).
