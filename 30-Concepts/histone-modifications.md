# Histone modifications

> Post-translational modifications of histone tails — methylation, acetylation, ubiquitylation, phosphorylation — that demarcate functional chromatin states.

## Definition

Modifications occur predominantly on histone H3 and H4 lysines and arginines. Mark-state mapping:
- **H3K4me3**: active promoters
- **H3K4me1**: enhancers (alone = primed; + H3K27ac = active; + H3K27me3 = poised)
- **H3K27ac**: active promoters and enhancers
- **H3K36me3**: actively transcribed gene bodies
- **H3K27me3**: facultative heterochromatin (Polycomb-repressed)
- **H3K9me3**: constitutive heterochromatin

## Why it matters

Histone marks are the substrate for cell-type-specific gene regulation. Profile them and you know which regions are active, primed, repressed, or silenced. Single-cell methods (scChIC-seq, scCUT&Tag, sciCUT&Tag, scChIX-seq, scEpi²-seq) extend this to individual cells.
