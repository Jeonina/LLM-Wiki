# Lawrence A. Loeb

> University of Washington (Seattle) faculty whose lab developed Duplex Sequencing — the foundational ultra-accurate NGS protocol that recovers somatic mutation frequencies below 10⁻⁸/bp.

## Profile

Pathologist and biochemist. Lab homepage: github.com/loeblab. Long-standing focus on the mutator phenotype in cancer and on developing methods to measure rare somatic mutations.

## Mentions

- **2026-05-12** — Co-senior author on [[10-Summaries/kennedy-2014-duplex-protocol]] (Kennedy et al. 2014 Nature Protocols), the reference Duplex Sequencing methodology.
