# Lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD)

> The most common subtype of non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC). Driven by EGFR, KRAS, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET, RET, NTRK mutations among others. Strong somatic methylation alterations characterize tumor subclones.

> [!abstract] 한눈에
> LUAD는 비소세포폐암에서 가장 흔한 아형이며, EGFR·KRAS 등 여러 driver 돌연변이와 subclone 수준의 methylation 변화가 특징이다. SpliCOOL-seq는 primary LUAD 조직에서 methylation biomarker와 tumor subclone의 가속된 epigenetic aging을 찾았다 ([[10-Summaries/shen-2026-splicool-seq|SpliCOOL-seq]]).

## Examples

- [[10-Summaries/shen-2026-splicool-seq|SpliCOOL-seq]] applies SpliCOOL-seq to primary LUAD tissue to identify methylation biomarkers (FAM124B, SFN, OR7E47P) and accelerated epigenetic aging in tumor subclones.
