# Leif S. Ludwig

> Berlin-based mitochondrial-genetics researcher who co-developed **mtscATAC-seq** (with Caleb Lareau, 2021) for joint single-cell mtDNA + chromatin profiling, enabling mitochondrial-variant-based lineage tracing in human hematopoiesis and disease.
