# Cardilla et al. 2025 — spatial DNA methylome + transcriptome

> Thesis: spatial omics has covered transcriptome and protein but not DNA methylation, the canonical epigenetic regulator. Cardilla et al. introduce a method for **whole-genome spatial co-profiling of DNA methylation and the transcriptome** in the same tissue section at **near single-cell resolution**. Applied to mouse embryogenesis and postnatal mouse brain.

## Key claims (abstract + intro)

- **First spatial whole-genome DNA methylome method** — fills the gap left by spatial transcriptomics methods (Visium, Stereo-seq, etc.).
- **Bimodal output per tissue spot**: DNA methylation + matched mRNA expression at near single-cell resolution.
- Applied to **mouse embryogenesis (two stages)** + **postnatal mouse brain** → DNA-RNA bimodal tissue maps.
- **Reveals spatial coordination of methylation and expression** — concordance and distinction patterns highlight synergistic cell-identity definition.
- **Developmental dynamics**: integrating maps from two embryonic stages reconstructs methylome+transcriptome evolution underlying mammalian embryogenesis.
- Cell-type- and region-specific methylation-mediated transcriptional regulation now mappable in spatial context.

## Why this matters

Pioneers **spatial DNA methylation** as a measurable modality. Complements spatial multi-omic methods (Stereo-seq, Slide-seq, Visium HD) by adding the epigenetic layer that has been missing. Anchors the spatial-multiomics axis of the wiki and bridges to single-cell methylome methods (Guo 2013, Smallwood 2014, SIMPLE-seq).
