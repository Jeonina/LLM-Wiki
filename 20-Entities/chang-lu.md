# Chang Lu

> Virginia Tech, chemical engineering. Builds microfluidic platforms for low-input and single-cell epigenomics.

## Mentions

- **2026-08-13** — Corresponding author of [[zhang-2023-drop-bs]] (Drop-BS), a droplet-microfluidic single-cell bisulfite sequencing platform reaching up to 10,000 cells in 2 days across three microfluidic devices. Its most transferable finding is chemical rather than mechanical: **bisulfite conversion performed inside droplets yields 9× more library than the same conversion in bulk**, at 99.0% conversion rate.
