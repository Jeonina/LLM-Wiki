# Jop Kind

> Group leader at the **Hubrecht Institute (KNAW)** and Oncode Institute, Utrecht. PI of the lab that turned bulk DamID into a workhorse single-cell method for protein–DNA contact mapping. Specialty: cell-to-cell heterogeneity in nuclear architecture — especially genome–nuclear-lamina interactions.

## Method lineage

- **Kind 2013** (Cell 153, with van Steensel) — single-cell dynamics of genome–nuclear-lamina interactions; foundational paper.
- **Kind 2015** (Cell 163, with van Steensel) — first genome-wide single-cell DamID; FACS + 384-well + barcoded PCR.
- **Rooijers 2019** (Nat Biotech, [[10-Summaries/rooijers-2019-scdamt-seq]]) — scDam&T-seq: joint protein–DNA + transcriptome in same cell via T7-IVT linear amplification.
- **de Luca & Kind 2021** ([[10-Summaries/de-luca-2021-scdamid-protocol]]) — canonical bench protocol chapter for scDamID.
