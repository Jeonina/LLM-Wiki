# Wenfei Jin

> Group leader at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), Shenzhen; developed scDNase-seq — the first method for genome-wide DNase I hypersensitivity in single cells — and the scNOMeRe-seq triple-omics platform (accessibility + methylation + RNA).

## Profile

- Affiliation: Department of Biology, SUSTech (Shenzhen)
- Research focus: single-cell epigenomics, chromatin accessibility, multi-omics integration, leukemia
- Methodological signature: scDNase-seq (Nature 2015), scNOMeRe-seq, FFPE single-cell methods

## Mentions

- **2026-05-19** — [[10-Summaries/wang-2023-multimodal-review]]: corresponding author. Comprehensive review of single-cell multimodal sequencing methods and integration tools.

## Positions and claims

- Single-cell mono-omics data often contains *hidden* additional modalities — scRNA-seq can yield CNV, allele-specific expression, and somatic SNVs; scDNase-seq can yield TF-binding-disrupting SNVs ([[10-Summaries/wang-2023-multimodal-review]]).
- Joint profiling of >3 modalities from the same cell may be fundamentally limited by molecule loss; future progress will come more from computational integration than from cramming modalities into one assay ([[10-Summaries/wang-2023-multimodal-review]]).
