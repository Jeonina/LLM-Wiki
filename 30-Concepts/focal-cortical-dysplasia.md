# Focal cortical dysplasia (FCD)

> A focal malformation of cortical development caused by somatic gain-of-function or loss-of-function mutations in [[30-Concepts/mtor-pathway]] genes during neural-progenitor expansion. Major cause of pharmacoresistant epilepsy in children.

> [!abstract] 한눈에
> Focal cortical dysplasia는 신경 전구세포 증식 중에 생긴 mTOR pathway 유전자의 somatic mutation으로 생기는 국소 피질 발생 기형이고, 소아 난치성 뇌전증의 주요 원인이다. Mouse model에서는 같은 *Pik3ca* 변이라도 어느 세포 유형에 생기느냐에 따라 표현형이 달라졌다 ([[10-Summaries/bizzotto-2022-brain-mosaicism-review|Bizzotto 2022]]).

## Definition

FCD lesions show abnormal "cytomegalic dysmorphic neurons" and "balloon cells" with hyperactive mTOR signaling. The size and severity of the lesion depend on the developmental timing of the mutation: early hits cause hemimegalencephaly (HME, whole-hemisphere); later hits cause focal FCD2.

## Why it matters

Somatic activating mutations in *AKT3, PIK3CA, RHEB, MTOR* or loss-of-function mutations in *TSC1, TSC2, DEPDC5, PTEN, NPRL2, NPRL3* account for 50–60% of FCD2/HME cases. Surgical resection of the lesion treats the epilepsy. mTOR-inhibitor drugs (everolimus) are under trial.

## Examples

- Pik3ca^H1047R restricted to excitatory neurons (Emx1-Cre) causes severe megalencephaly; restricted to inhibitory neurons (Nkx2.1-Cre) does not — **cell type matters as much as the mutation** ([[10-Summaries/bizzotto-2022-brain-mosaicism-review]]).
