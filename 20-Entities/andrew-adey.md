# Andrew C. Adey

> Oregon Health & Science University. Extends [[combinatorial-indexing|combinatorial indexing]] to new modalities — most consequentially to bisulfite sequencing.

## Mentions

- **2026-08-13** — Corresponding author of [[mulqueen-2018-sci-met]] (sci-MET), which brought combinatorial indexing to single-cell whole-genome bisulfite sequencing using **cytosine-depleted transposome adaptors** that survive bisulfite treatment. The headline result was an alignment rate of 68 ± 8%, approaching bulk, against the 25 ± 20% typical of one-cell-per-well scWGBS.
- **2026-08-13** — The sci-MET line continues in [[nichols-2022-scimet-v2]].
- **2026-10-07** — Corresponding author of [[10-Summaries/rylaarsdam-2025-amethyst]] (Amethyst R package for sciMET data; processed 145,219 sciMETv3 cells and found neuron-like hyper-mCH in astrocytes and oligodendrocytes).
- **2026-10-07** — corresponding author in [[10-Summaries/nichols-2025-scimetv3]] (sciMETv3: third-tier ligation barcoding for atlas-scale single-cell methylomes, >140,000 human MFG cells; sciMET+ATAC co-assay; discloses sciMET patents and Scale Biosciences advisory role).
- **2026-10-07** — Corresponding author of [[10-Summaries/acharya-2024-scimet-cap]] (sciMET-cap: off-the-shelf hybrid capture on sciMETv2 libraries; similar human-cortex clustering from ~350 M reads versus ~7 billion without capture; declares patents and Scale Biosciences equity covering sciMETv2).
