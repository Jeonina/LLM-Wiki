# Ferhat Ay

> La Jolla Institute for Immunology. Statistical methods for chromatin conformation data; author of the Fit-Hi-C line.

## Mentions

- **2026-08-13** — Corresponding author of [[chakraborty-2022-dchic]] (dcHiC), which makes differential [[chromatin-compartments|compartment]] analysis across ≥2 Hi-C datasets tractable via automated PC-and-sign selection, quantile normalisation, and a Mahalanobis distance with χ² significance. Its most consequential finding is a category rather than a benchmark: **~26% of significant compartment changes involve no A↔B flip**, and those within-compartment changes track lamin B1, replication timing, and expression just as flips do.
- dcHiC also supplies unusually concrete operational limits — 10-kb differential analysis is false-positive-prone (median 751 spurious bins in replicate-vs-replicate), and samples differing >2–3× in depth should not be compared ([[chakraborty-2022-dchic]]).
