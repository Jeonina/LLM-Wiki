# SMaHT Network

> An NIH Common Fund consortium (Somatic Mosaicism across Human Tissues) launched in 2023 to catalog somatic mutations across human tissues throughout the lifespan. Hub: Brigham and Women's Hospital / Broad / Boston Children's.

## Profile

- **Single Cell Focus Group**: PTA-based scDNA-seq on diverse tissues, lead investigators include Christopher Walsh, Peter Park, Flora Vaccarino, Alexej Abyzov, Dan Landau.
- **Duplex Sequencing Focus Group**: cross-platform benchmarking of duplex methods (CODEC, CompDup, HiDEF-seq, NanoSeq, ppmSeq, VISTA-seq).
